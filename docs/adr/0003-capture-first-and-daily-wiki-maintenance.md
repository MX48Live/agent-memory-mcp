# Capture first and maintain a wiki daily

> The automatic Codex client workflow in this decision is superseded by [ADR 0004](0004-notion-time-logs.md). Server architecture/history requirements remain in force.

Accepted on 2026-11-08. Supersedes the prohibition on timestamp-based precedence in ADR 0001 and its requirement to turn every unexplained changed fact into a conflict; the OpenAI-only storage and recovery decisions remain in force. The user wants fast task completion without prior-memory deduplication or contradiction scans, while retaining the [LLM Wiki](https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f) pattern of accumulated, maintained knowledge.

Completion uses Sol to extract assertions from the new curated input only, then commits immutable captures and the task log with operation-local integrity checks. Reads combine persisted wiki pages with fresh captures and select the newest recorded current assertion of the same scoped fact. Plans, historical claims, and uncertain statements do not supersede established current state; uncertain identity or ordering remains visible. Retries, wiki generation, and hosted legacy migration preserve the assertion's original recorded time.

An external ChatGPT/Dot caller runs daily wiki maintenance at 03:00 Asia/Bangkok. Each project's first call freezes that maintenance job's source scope; all bounded batches and retries reuse its task key until that scope is complete. Each batch compiles at most 20 captures with one Sol call, persists exact source coverage, and can resume from a fresh Worker instance. Hosted legacy knowledge retains its original documents, logs, and operation-plan time. The daily workflow resumes unfinished jobs first, continues batches while coverage advances across projects and global, then performs read-only audit. Indexing/no-progress stalls receive one resume before deferral; advancing batches are not stalled saves. It reports ambiguities, incomplete coverage, and failures rather than automatically deleting records or guessing a winner. Semantic review is bounded and model-assisted, not a guarantee of detecting every contradiction.

All durable captures, wiki revisions, logs, recovery material, and operation plans remain on OpenAI Files and Vector Stores. There is no D1, R2, separate catalogue, local database, or persistent background worker, and no import from the archived local vault. This trades immediate whole-corpus reconciliation for a temporarily less integrated wiki; fresh captures must therefore remain queryable before maintenance. Save status confirms storage integrity, not universal factual correctness or completed wiki synthesis.
